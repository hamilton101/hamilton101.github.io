Dr. Matthew Hamilton joins project lead Dr. Oscar De Silva and Dr. Rocky Taylor on a sea ice monitoring project awarded **$238,048** through the Canada Foundation for Innovation's John R. Evans Leaders Fund.

The project, *Next-Generation Multispectral Sensing Station for Real-Time Sea Ice Monitoring in Coastal Newfoundland and Labrador*, is one of six Memorial University projects featured in the Gazette's October 1, 2026 story, "High-tech breakthroughs."

[Read the full story in the Gazette](https://gazette.mun.ca/research/high-tech-breakthroughs/).
