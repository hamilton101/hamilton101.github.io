Work on this project so far focuses on automating the reading of mammography
images using artificial intelligence. The goal is to reduce radiologist
workload while improving cancer detection rates.

## Collaborators

This work is being carried out in collaboration with Memorial University's
Faculty of Medicine, Newfoundland and Labrador Health Services (NL Health
Services), and researchers at Anqing Normal University in China.
