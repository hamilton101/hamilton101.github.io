This project explores intelligent simulation and training technologies for
ship operations in Arctic ice. The work aims to support safer decision-making,
more efficient operations, and more sustainable marine transportation in
challenging northern environments.

## Collaborators

This work is being carried out in collaboration with the National Research
Council Canada (NRC) and Virtual Marine Technology.
