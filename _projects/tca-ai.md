## Overview

TCA-AI brings together data integration, ocean data assimilation, and artificial
intelligence to support climate research. The project combines observations
from sources such as the Canadian Integrated Ocean Observing System, ocean
carbon atlases, and industry partners to advance deep learning for climate
modelling.

Research goals include reducing uncertainty in climate predictions, developing
models that handle real-world data quality challenges, improving environmental
DNA analysis, and supporting automated monitoring of ocean biodiversity. The
project also explores sustainable approaches to AI models.

## Leak detection at the VAC Lab

The VAC Lab's leak detection research is part of TCA-AI. This work contributes
to the project's broader research on artificial intelligence and ocean data.

## Collaboration

Matthew Hamilton at Memorial University is part of the project team alongside
Christopher Whidden, Tushar Sharma, and Evangelos Milios at Dalhousie University.
